# So, let me tell you a story.

Back in February of 2026 (if i recall correctly), I announced that I was making a mod. That mod was using a decomp (this one).
Me, having been part of the Pizza Tower community from mid-2025 onwards, had just joined the FB3 community (and the FNaF community later on in mid-2026) and was just getting acquainted with the community.
But tragedy struck when I realized UTMT couldn't export rooms. I had to find a suitable decompilating software capable of decompiling the game, and fast, but alas, I could not find a match.
And so, I called off the decomp in Winter 2026, blissfully unaware that fate had other plans...
But then, as of writing this (15/9/2026), yesterday, I was deep diving in the depths of the Pizza Tower Fan's Paradise (Check it out [here.](https://rentry.co/pizzatowerparadise)), when i saw it. The perfect decomp software. It's name? the UnderAnalyzer Decompiler.
So, I downloaded it, tested it on the game, and sure enough? It worked.

This decomp/engine is the result. Thank you for everything.

# HOW TO USE

You will need:

- This decomp
- Gamemaker 2023.11.1.129 (Get it [here.](https://releases.gamemaker.io/release-notes/2023/11)) or LTS 2022.0 onwards.
- [This link](https://mega.nz/file/ClAD1YpJ#5pJCh1-i8RMVs3tSRIaAEX5x_fJOKiC-Dw1HdkbGr4E)  (Github doesn't take files over 25mb)

1. Download the decomp and every requirement mentioned above.
2. Delete the following files: `README.md, script.log`.
3. Move the files inside `innerworkings_files` to `innerworkings_main`
4. Open `Frickbears3.yyp` in Gamemaker.
5. Enjoy!
